# OPEN WORLD palettes.
#
# DMG shades: 0 white, 1 light grey, 2 dark grey, 3 black.  BGP byte = s0 | s1<<2 | s2<<4 | s3<<6
# where sN is the shade shown for colour index N.  Colour 0 is LIGHT and is white in every phase.
#
#   dawn  0xA4  0->0 1->1 2->2 3->2   haze: nothing is black yet; the land is lifted and soft.
#   day   0xE4  identity.
#   dusk  0xF4  0->0 1->1 2->3 3->3   long shadows: detail hardens into black silhouettes, the
#                                     sea (colour 2) goes dark, the ground still holds the light.
#   night 0xF8  0->0 1->2 2->3 3->3   the ground sinks one step to dark grey (the terrain stays
#                                     legible: paths, sand, snow, clearings), everything that has
#                                     form becomes black silhouette, and only colour 0 is bright:
#                                     fires, beacons, stars, glints, the Heart.
# On the band the sky is colour 1 and the far land colour 3, so the skyline survives every phase
# (at night: black mountains under a dark-grey sky, stars in white).
#
# OBP0 (figures) 1->0 2->2 3->3 by day / dawn (0xE0); dusk/night 2->1 (0xD0): the cloak's rim
#      catches the light so the wanderer's black silhouette stays readable on dark ground.
# OBP1 (lights)  1->0 2->1 3->2 (0x90) always: pips, lantern glow, rain, Watchers, lit beacon.
@dmg dawn  bgp=0xA4 obp0=0xE0 obp1=0x90
@dmg day   bgp=0xE4 obp0=0xE0 obp1=0x90
@dmg dusk  bgp=0xF4 obp0=0xD0 obp1=0x90
@dmg night bgp=0xF8 obp0=0xD0 obp1=0x90
